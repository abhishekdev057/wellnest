How it works

Four simple steps to a healthier routine

No complicated plans or clinical tracking — just friendly goals, honest logging and a little healthy competition.

  1. 01

    Join a challenge

    Pick from movement, hydration, mindfulness and healthy-habit challenges designed by your organizer.

  2. 02

    Log your activities

    Record walks, water, meditation or stretch breaks in seconds — from any page, on any device.

  3. 03

    Earn points & streaks

    Every valid activity earns points. Stay active on consecutive days to grow your streak.

  4. 04

    Celebrate progress

    Complete challenges for bonus points, unlock badges and see how you rank with your community.
